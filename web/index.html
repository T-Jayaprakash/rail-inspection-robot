    How it works

    A rail-mounted robot drives the track by itself. A down-looking camera finds both rails, measures the gauge (1676 mm broad gauge, ±10 mm tolerance) and detects rail-head cracks with a morphological black-hat filter plus a shape test that rejects rust stains. A front camera and an ultrasonic sensor slow the robot for obstacles and stop it at 2 m.

    Each defect is logged with chainage, GPS and a photo. Camera images on this page come from the project's track simulator; every box, measurement and decision is made by the real inspection code.
